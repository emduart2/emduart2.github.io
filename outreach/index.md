# Outreach

<p class="lead">Mathematics is more fun when it is shared. Here are podcasts, videos and projects that bring my field to a wider audience.</p>

## Podcasts

- **[TensorVoices](https://tensorvoices.de)** · A five-episode podcast of conversations about tensors with enthusiastic guests, co-hosted with Thomas Kahle.
- **[Modellansatz](https://www.math.kit.edu/ianm4/seite/ma-algebraic-geometry/de)** · Interview about algebraic geometry and its applications.
- **[Interviews for Future](https://interviews-4-future.podigee.io/48-i4f)** · Interview about mathematics and science.

## Videos

- **[Video abstract](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CccVNRFmR1I)** · Representing discrete context-specific causal models, with Liam Solus.
- **[Algebraic Statistics Online Seminar](https://sites.google.com/view/algstatsonline/past-talks-and-recordings?authuser=0#h.cst9zjs53ai8)** · Recorded talk on the algebraic geometry of discrete interventional models.
- **[Virtual Rigidity Seminar](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b-tVNwWr3Fg)** · Recorded talk on rigidity of quasicrystal frameworks.

## Hands-on mathematics

- **[Building frameworks](https://emduart2.github.io/framework-rigidity/)** · A webpage explaining how to build rigid frameworks in real life.
